# Purchase order process

## Lifecycle
A purchase order starts as a draft, becomes ordered when it is sent to the supplier, and is marked received once the goods arrive and are checked. Orders can be cancelled while in draft or ordered status.

## Approval rules
Purchase orders above 5,000 need approval from the procurement manager. Orders above 20,000 also need finance approval. A draft order cannot be sent to the supplier until it is approved.

## Late deliveries
A purchase order is late when its expected date has passed and it is still in ordered status. Procurement should contact the supplier after 3 days of delay and escalate to the procurement manager after 7 days.

## Receiving goods
When goods arrive, the warehouse compares the delivered quantity with the ordered quantity. Any shortage or damage must be recorded before the order is marked received, so the supplier can be asked for a credit note.
