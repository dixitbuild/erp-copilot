# Sales order process

## Lifecycle
A sales order moves through these statuses: draft, confirmed, shipped, delivered. A draft order is still being edited and has not been committed to the customer. Once the sales team confirms it, stock is reserved and the order counts as open. Shipping happens when the goods leave the warehouse, and delivered means the customer has received them.

## Cancellation policy
An order can be cancelled freely while it is in draft or confirmed status. Once an order is shipped it can no longer be cancelled; the customer must raise a return instead. Cancelled orders are excluded from all revenue and open-order totals.

## Revenue recognition
Revenue is counted only when an order is shipped or delivered. Draft and confirmed orders are treated as pipeline, not revenue.

## Discounts and approval
Orders with a total amount above 10,000 need approval from a sales manager before they can be confirmed. Discounts above 15 percent also need manager approval.
