# ERP glossary

## Open order
An open sales order is one in draft or confirmed status. An open purchase order is one in draft or ordered status. Open orders represent work that is still to be done.

## Lead time
Lead time is the number of days between placing an order and receiving or delivering it. For purchase orders it measures supplier speed; for sales orders it measures how fast we serve customers.

## Backorder
A backorder is an order line that cannot be shipped yet because stock is not available. Work orders are often created to cover backorders.

## Unit price
Unit price is the total amount of an order divided by its quantity.
